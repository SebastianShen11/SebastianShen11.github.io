THE RIGHT FIT

Size guide

Measure while your pet is standing naturally. Use a soft tape and allow two fingers beneath it. If between sizes, choose the larger size.

01

Neck

Measure around the base of the neck where a collar naturally sits.

02

Chest

Measure around the widest point, just behind the front legs.

03

Back

Measure from the base of the neck to the base of the tail.
