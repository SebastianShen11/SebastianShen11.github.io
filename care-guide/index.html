FIELD NOTES

Care, considered.

A little regular care helps well-made pet pieces stay comfortable, useful and beautiful for longer.

TEXTILES

Cotton gauze

Fasten closures, wash gently in cool water and reshape while damp. Dry naturally away from direct heat.

LEATHER

Full-grain leather

Wipe after muddy walks and allow to dry naturally. Use a small amount of neutral leather balm from time to time.

DINING

Ceramic & timber

Hand wash ceramic inserts with mild detergent. Wipe timber stands promptly and never leave them soaking.
